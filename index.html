Pricing & how it works

Free to list. You only pay when it actually works.

No monthly fees, no hidden charges — Pettify makes money the same way you do: when a pet finds its home.

Breeders & vendors
9% + ₦50

Charged only on a successful sale of a pet, accessory or vet booking. Listing itself is free, for life.

Buyers
1%

A small service fee on your purchase. Delivery fees are set by the seller and shown before checkout.

01

Create a free account

Sign up with an email or Google — no fees, no waiting period.

02

Connect your bank details

Add the account your payouts should land in, verified once, not every sale.

03

List your pets & accessories

Add photos, breed, age and vaccination details — full records sell faster.

04

Withdraw your earnings

Once a sale clears, your payout (minus the fee) is yours to withdraw.
