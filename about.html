Why We Built Pettify

Finding a healthy, purebred pet or genuine pet supplies used to mean dealing with unverified social media posts, safety doubts, and uncertain delivery.

We created Pettify to solve this problem. Every seller on Pettify goes through an identity verification process, every listing is inspected, and every purchase is backed by our secure platform.
